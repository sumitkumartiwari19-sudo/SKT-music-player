🎵 SKT Music Player

Your Music. Your Experience.

A modern Android music player designed for a smooth, personalized, and enjoyable offline listening experience. Explore your music library, organize your favourite tracks, create playlists, enjoy synchronized lyrics, and customize your listening experience with beautiful themes and fluid animations.

Current Version: "v2.0.2"

✨ Features

🎧 Music Playback & Library

- Play music stored on your Android device.
- Browse and manage your local music library.
- Search for songs quickly.
- Enjoy a dedicated full-screen music player.
- Access your favourite and recently played tracks.
- Browse music folders and discover your collection.
- Enjoy a personalized listening experience with customizable playback screens.

📚 Smart Playlists

Organize your music with dedicated playlist screens and automatically maintained collections.

- Favourites — Quickly access your favourite songs.
- Recently Played — Return to tracks you've listened to recently.
- Most Played — Find your frequently played songs.
- Recently Added — Discover music added to your library.
- Custom Playlists — Create and manage your own collections.
- Play & Shuffle — Play tracks in order or shuffle a playlist.
- Song Management — Remove individual songs from playlists.
- Playlist Management — Delete playlists when no longer needed.
- Home Shortcuts — Open the correct Favourites and Recent playlists directly.

🎨 Six Beautiful App Themes

Choose the appearance that suits your style.

Theme| Description
System Default| Follows your device's theme
Dark| A comfortable dark interface
AMOLED Black| A deep black appearance
Light| A clean, bright interface
Midnight Blue| A rich blue-toned appearance
Frosted Glass| A translucent, softly blurred aesthetic

Dynamic Artwork Colors: Optionally adapt the app's colours to the artwork of the currently playing song.

🎬 Smooth Animations & Gestures

Enjoy fluid transitions throughout the music-listening experience.

- 4 UI animation styles for interface transitions.
- 6 mini-player swipe animation styles for changing songs.
- Swipe the mini player left or right to switch tracks.
- Expand the mini player into the full player with a smooth, finger-following animation.
- 3 mini-to-full-player transition styles to choose from.
- Shared Axis is the default UI animation style.
- Fade Drift is the default mini-player swipe animation.
- Container Morph is the default mini-to-full-player transition.
- Improved swipe-up detection for opening the full player.

Additional animation options are available in Settings.

❄️ Refined Frosted Glass UI

- Darker, softly blurred mini player.
- Matching frosted glass navigation bar.
- Improved text readability over translucent surfaces.
- A refined interface designed to complement the selected theme.

🎤 Lyrics Experience

Enjoy a dedicated lyrics interface while listening to your music.

- Access the lyrics screen from the music player.
- Switch between the song and lyrics views where supported.

📱 Home Screen Music Widget

Control your music directly from your Android home screen.

- Vinyl-style artwork.
- Music progress and seek bar.
- Playback buttons.
- Support for following the app theme or choosing a preferred widget theme.

🛠️ Quality & Usability Improvements

- Fixed app icon clipping on round and circular launchers.
- Improved playlist navigation.
- Fixed Favourites and Recent home shortcuts.
- Refined player gestures and animations.
- Updated the application version to v2.0.2.

📥 Download & Installation

Download the APK

1. Visit the "SKT Music Player Releases" (https://github.com/sumitkumartiwari19-sudo/SKT-music-player/releases) page.
2. Open the latest published release.
3. Download the APK asset.
4. Open the downloaded APK on your Android device.
5. If prompted, allow installation from the source used to download the APK.
6. Complete installation and launch SKT Music Player.

Note: The v2.0.2 APK must be published on GitHub Releases for users to download that version directly.

Updating the App

To update an existing installation, download the newer APK and install it over the current app. Android requires a compatible application ID and signing certificate, along with a higher version code.
